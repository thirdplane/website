description: "Two explorations into human-AI creative collaboration — making intent inspectable, tunable, and yours."
longDescription: "These works share a premise: natural language is a lossy medium for conveying aesthetic intent to AI. Generative Remix sidesteps articulation entirely — you point at something tasteful and the system extracts the parameters. Intent Configuration Interface catches language mid-translation, materializing what the model heard as an editable intermediate artifact. Together they propose a different model for human-AI co-creation: one where interpretation is visible, correctable, and portable."
curatorNote: "I'm interested in the gap between what people mean and what AI hears. Both of these projects try to close that gap without forcing you to become more precise — instead, they make the system's interpretation a first-class thing you can see and touch."
