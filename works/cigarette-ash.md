## About This Piece

> An AI-generated meditation on impermanence. Cigarette ash accumulating and dispersing, rendered through Sora's emergent understanding of physics and time.

A study in AI-generated physics and temporal decay. Sora interprets the slow accumulation of cigarette ash: the delicate balance before collapse, the scatter of particles, the entropy of form.

What draws me to this output is not its realism but its *almost*-ness: the uncanny way generative models approximate physical phenomena they've never experienced. The ash falls convincingly until it doesn't. The physics holds until it slips into something dreamlike.

This is AI as collaborator in observing the world. It is not replacing the camera, but offering a parallel lens.
