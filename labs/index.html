Where I can help

  • Your scores look good, but important failures still get through.

    An assessment of what the evaluation misses, with supporting examples and priorities for correction.

  • Your team can’t reliably tell whether a change is better.

    A working evaluation with relevant cases and criteria for comparing models, prompts, or workflows.

  • Manual review is slowing development.

    A workflow that automates suitable checks and routes uncertain cases, with supporting evidence, to human review.

Consistency needs evidence

Two questions guide the work: are judgments consistent under comparable conditions, and does evidence connect them to the intended outcome?

How to read the framework

Disagreement may occur between AI evaluators, between AI and human reviewers, or between people. These are places to investigate, not positions on the chart.

  • AI–AI: examine variability and bias.
  • AI–human: inspect mismatches and calibrate review.
  • Human–human: clarify criteria and investigate disputed evidence.

The framework is Thirdplane Labs’ synthesis, informed by Zheng et al. on model-judge biases, Jiang and de Marneffe on human disagreement, and Anthropic on complementary evaluation methods.

How an engagement works

We start with one decision your team needs to make, then agree on the scope, relevant examples, and a useful deliverable. I work with your existing product and evaluation setup, and hand over the agreed work with findings, limitations, and next steps.
