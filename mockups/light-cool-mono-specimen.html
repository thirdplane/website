Data — divergence table (color lives here, and only here)

Chrome (headers, rules, row labels) stays ink. Judge identity is the row label — nothing else. The one hue in the system marks a single fact: this judge diverged from the human baseline on this category. That's the Labs translation of Variation B's "the work is the color; the site is the gallery": here, the divergence is the color — not the judges themselves, which don't scale past a handful of hues at six-wide.
