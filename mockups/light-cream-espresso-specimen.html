Artificial Creativity

Collection · 04 works

Natural language is a lossy medium for conveying intent to a machine.

These works catch language mid-translation — materializing what the model heard as an editable intermediate artifact. Together they propose a different model for human–AI co-creation: one where interpretation is visible, correctable, and portable.
