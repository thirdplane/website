Curator's Note

These works share a premise: natural language is a lossy medium for conveying user intent to AI. UI and shader components are the focus because they contain nuances that are hard to describe unless one knows the specific requirements — both technical and ‘vibes-based’.

Together they propose a different model for human–AI co-creation: one where interpretation is visible, correctable, and portable.
